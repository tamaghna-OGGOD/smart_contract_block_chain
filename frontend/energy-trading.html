This high-level architecture diagram shows how energy producers, consumers, and validators interact through the blockchain network. Smart meters capture production and consumption data, which is recorded on the Hyperledger Fabric blockchain using smart contracts. Validators use a Proof of Authority consensus mechanism to verify transactions, while oracles provide external data verification.

Smart Contracts & Business Logic

Key Smart Contract Functions

  • CreateToken: Issues new energy tokens based on verified production
  • TransferToken: Transfers token ownership between accounts
  • GetAllTokens: Retrieves all tokens from the ledger
  • ReadToken: Retrieves a specific token's details
